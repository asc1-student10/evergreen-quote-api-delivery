# Evergreen Quote API: Proposed Roadmap

## Round 1: Stabilize the service
**Goal:** Make the service dependable and ready for a production-readiness assessment.

- Confirm the rate table and define who approves future rate changes.
- Add operational monitoring for availability, errors, and response times.
- Document service ownership, support expectations, and recovery procedures.
- Review security and data-handling requirements before any production use.

**Exit criteria:** The sponsor approves the operational and security gaps to address before a production launch.

## Round 2: Prepare for partner use
**Goal:** Enable selected partners to integrate with the API safely and consistently.

- Confirm partner use cases and prioritize an initial pilot.
- Define authentication, authorization, usage limits, and partner onboarding.
- Publish API documentation, error guidance, and versioning expectations.
- Test the integration with a representative partner workflow.

**Exit criteria:** A pilot partner can request and retrieve quotes under agreed access and support controls.

## Round 3: Launch and learn
**Goal:** Release to an agreed production audience and use results to guide improvements.

- Complete production deployment, resilience, backup, and recovery planning.
- Define launch measures, such as quote completion, response time, and error rate.
- Run a limited rollout and review customer and partner feedback.
- Prioritize the next improvements using observed outcomes.

**Exit criteria:** The sponsor reviews launch results and agrees on the next delivery priorities.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This roadmap is a proposal, not a commitment. Production deployment depends on sponsor approval, security and operational readiness, and confirmed partner needs. Revisit sequencing and scope after the capstone review.
