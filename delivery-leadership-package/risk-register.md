## How I'll use this register

I will review this register at the daily check-in and update it when a risk changes, a mitigation is completed, or a new blocker appears. I am accountable as Delivery Lead for making sure risks have owners, follow-up, and timely escalation; technical owners remain accountable for investigation and fixes, while the instructor or VM administrator handles issues requiring elevated access. I will share the register with my project pair and instructor and use the escalation triggers to keep risks visible.
